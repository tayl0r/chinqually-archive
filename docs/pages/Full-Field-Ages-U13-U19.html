Full Field (U13-U19)

Chinqually Booters participates along with other local clubs in the Thurston County Youth Soccer Association (TCYSA).

Age groups U13 through U19 play regular, 11-a-side soccer on full-size fields. Practices are set by the individual team coaches and take place at local schools and parks.

Full Field teams can expect some travel to Lewis, Mason and Grays Harbor Counties. Every team has four home games in Thurston County and four away games. Away games may be in Thurston County, or the counties listed above.

You can find match Rules here | Age Chart

There are 3 Seasons of Play: Spring, Summer, and Fall. Currently available programs can be found on the Registration Page.

Each Fall of every year, a new season begins and every child advances to the next age group.
