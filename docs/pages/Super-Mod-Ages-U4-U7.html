Super Mod (Ages U4-U7)

The Super Mod program is for U4 to U7 age groups.
Matches are played on smaller fields with 3 players on the field for each team - no goalkeepers.
Super Mod introduces children to the game of soccer, while concentrating on developing skills and having fun.
All players must wear shinguards. Cleats are optional but highly recommended. 

Full set of Match Rules can be found here | Age Chart

Players receive a jersey and a soccer ball for $75 per season.

Questions and concerns can be directed to the Super Mod Coordinator at supermod@chinqually.org or [phone removed]

  • Summer - June 21 to August 3

LOCATION:

Matches are played at Rainier Vista Park in Lacey off of Ruddell and 45th

PRACTICES:

Practices are at 6 PM on Friday evening with a game on Saturday morning at 9 AM or 10:15 AM
