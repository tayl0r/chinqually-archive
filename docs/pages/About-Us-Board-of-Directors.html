About Us & Board of Directors

ABOUT US

The Chinqually Booters Soccer Club (CBSC) (Est. 1970) is a non-profit (501(c)3) recreational soccer organization that offers programs for youth from ages 4 to 19. Our Club is governed by a Board of Directors, which strives to provide a quality soccer program based on our organization's Vision and Mission Statement. Board meetings are held monthly, usually on the 2nd Monday of each month. See the calendar for location and time.

Board of Directors

As a member of the CBSC Board of Directors, we meet once a month to discuss various topics concerning our program. Our commitment to the program and community remains the highest of priorities in which we all strive to bring about a program that is safe, affordable, and is for everyone.
