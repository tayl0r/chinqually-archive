Mod (U8 - U12)


The Mod (modified soccer) program provides a development path leading up to full field 11-a-side play. Using smaller fields, smaller team sizes, and modified rules, the Mod program gives players more opportunities with the ball and involvement in the game, which enhances their skill development.

The progression in players on the field is:

  • U08 : 5-a-side (4 players + goalkeeper)
  • U09/U10 : 7-a-side (6 players + goalkeeper)
  • U11/U12 : 9-a-side (8 players + goalkeeper)

Chinqually is one of the clubs organized by Thurston County Youth Soccer. Our games are played against other Thurston County recreational clubs from Olympia, Tumwater, Yelm, Tenino and Rochester.

There are 3 Seasons of Play: Spring, Summer, and Fall. Currently available programs can be found on the Registration Page.

Questions and concerns can be directed to the Chinqually Manager at manager@chinqually.org
