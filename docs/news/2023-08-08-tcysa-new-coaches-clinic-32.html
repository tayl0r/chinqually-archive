TCYSA New coaches clinic

Last updated 2023-08-08

New to coaching? Coached a season and still have questions?

TCYSA is hosting a new coach clinic for all new (and newer) coaches who would like to learn how to run practices and games. Come join us at Rainier Vista on Tuesday, August 15 at 6:00, working with seasoned coaches and learning tips and tricks before the season starts.
