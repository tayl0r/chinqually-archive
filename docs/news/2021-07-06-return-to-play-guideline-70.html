RETURN TO PLAY GUIDELINE

Last updated 2021-07-06

HERE is the link to the Return to Play Guideline. Below are some of the highlights.

Soccer is defined as a Moderate Risk sport. 

As of 6/1/2021 we are in Phase 3

FACIAL COVERINGS REQUIRED FOR ALL COACHES, VOLUNTEER AND ATHLETES on the sidelines

Players are not required to wear  a mask while actively playing

Maximum 200 people allowed at competitions including spectators. For outdoor competitions, facilities or complexes with more than one field may have a maximum of 75 people per field. Spectators of different households are to remain 6 feet or more apart.

STAY HOME WHEN SICK OR IF A CLOSE CONTACT OF SOMEONE WITH COVID-19
