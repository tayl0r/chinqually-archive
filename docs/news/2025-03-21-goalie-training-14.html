Is your player interested in being a goalie? Already a goalie and would like some new tips? Please join other goalies for some training!

Goalie Training

Rainier Vista Soccer Field #5

March 29, 2025

10 am to 11:30 am  (U10 and younger)

12:00 to 1:30 pm  (U11 and older)
