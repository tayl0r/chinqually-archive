Metropolotan Park District

Last updated 2018-11-13

City of Lacey will have Proposition 1

on the ballot November 6, a vote to create the Metropolitan Park District within the City Limits of Lacey.

For our soccer playing families, what this means to us is the creation of soccer fields at Meridian Park at the north end of Lacey where there are currently no soccer fields.

And an Outdoor Sports Stadium as part of the Regional Athletic Complex Expansion.

City of Lacey is known for its beautiful parks and CBSC and TC United uses every soccer field the City of Lacey has.
