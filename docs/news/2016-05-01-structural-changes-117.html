Structural Changes

Last updated 2016-05-01

Chinqually Families,

 

Soccer in the United States has been going through significant structural changes over the past few years.  Birth year registration and other proposed local changes will impact our programs.   I wanted to let you know the Chinqually Board of Directors is currently reviewing our recreational program structure and program administration. Please look for future announcements regarding program changes.  Our recreational program has a long history in Thurston County and we look forward to providing recreational playing opportunities for many years to come.

 

If you have questions, concerns or suggestions please feel free to reach out to me personally, any of our board members or your child's coach.  

 

Coaches, the Chinqually Board will be holding a "Coaches Meeting" in the near future to discuss potential changes to the recreational program.

 

Our next meeting is scheduled for May 15, 2016 at 6:30 pm at the Franz Street Fire Station located in Lacey, WA.  The meeting is open to everyone.

 

Tony Ramos, President on Behalf of 

Chinqually Board of Directors
