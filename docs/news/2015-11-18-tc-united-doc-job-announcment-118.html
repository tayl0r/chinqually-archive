TC United DOC Job Announcment

Last updated 2015-11-18

TC United is seeking a Director of Coaching

Apply Online

Questions directed to [email removed]

Deadline is November 7th

Requirements:

  • Four year degree desired with experience in soccer club administration preferred.
  • B license preferred with desire to get A within three to five years.
  • Has or equivalent to 10 years’ experience as a college or professional player, or coaching competitively at the college, premier or higher.  
  • Comfortable with public speaking and facilitating meetings.
  • Strong collaborative traits with an ability to lead and work cooperatively.
  • Knowledge of sports psychology desired.

Duties and Roles

  • Will be the “face” and voice of the program with parents, players and the community.
  • Manages coaches, including assignment of duties and tracking coach and player development.
  • Builds a coaching and player development model.
  • Trains teams within TC United and Chinqually, but will not train own team.
  • Develops and promotes program camps and community clinics, including try outs.
  • Focus on competitive youth of TC united, building a foundation for the future starting with our youngest teams.
  • Available to train with Chinqually, ensuring recreational coaches and players have the means necessary to operate at the select and premier level.
