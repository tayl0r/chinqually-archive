TC United and CBSC Goalkeeper Coach Clinic

Refine Your Goalkeeping Coaching Techniques

TC United and CBSC Coaches:

TC United and CBSC cordially invite you to attend a Goalkeeper Coach’s Clinic, an event specially crafted to provide basics to advanced insights and methodologies for coaching goalkeepers. This clinic represents a valuable opportunity to enhance your understanding of the position under the guidance of an accomplished leader in the field.

**Event Details**

Date: Friday, June 6th

Time and Location: 6:00 PM - 7:30 PM at the Regional Athletic Complex Turf Field

Presenter: Kyle, Director of Coaching for TC United and CBSC

Credentials: Holder of the USSF A License, National Youth License, and USSF Club Technical Leadership License

Experience: Over 9 years of experience coaching collegiate goalkeepers

**What to Expect**

- A comprehensive overview of fundamental goalkeeping principles

- Practical strategies to effectively support goalkeepers during training and matches

- A series of concise, actionable exercises to implement with goalkeepers

Kindly note that this clinic is **exclusively intended for coaches** and is **not open to players**. If you would like to have a player attend to observe the training, you are welcome to do so.

We sincerely hope you will join us for this enriching and professional development opportunity.
