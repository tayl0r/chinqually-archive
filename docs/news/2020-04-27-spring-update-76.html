TCYSA – Covid 19 (Coronavirus) response for Spring Season 2020                                                                               

TCYSA and our member Clubs are committed to the safety of our players and their families.

In response to the Covid 19 outbreak and subsequent school and other closures we are taking the following steps:

TCYSA’s Spring Recreational Soccer season will be postponed. At this time, we plan to resume the Spring season with games beginning on May 2 and running through June 20. TCYSA’s goal is to host seven games during the modified season schedule. Practices are cancelled until the week of April 27. No teams may hold practice until that time. We will re-release a game revised game schedule later in April.

However, given how unprecedented this situation is, it is possible that these plans could change depending on the state of emergency at that time as well as the availability of fields for both practices and games.

TCYSA’s Board will meet again on April 19 to determine if it is necessary to revise our plan for a modified Spring season.

When the season resumes, we will provide coaches, players and families with any additional guidance on the need to maintain social distancing in accordance with advice from local health officials.

We appreciate the patience and understanding of all of our soccer coaches and families as we all work through this difficult and unsettling time. TCYSA’s Board felt that it was our best course of action to try to resume the season as a way to help our community and our kids get back to a normal routine. We are looking forward to getting back out on the grass with a soccer ball as soon as we can.

Until then, here is a link to some suggestions from the King County Public Health Department,  https://publichealthinsider.com/2020/03/14/community-is-our-anchor-during-covid-19/
