NTPS Partners in Education (PIE) Program

Community & Schools Together

Community engagement is a key to successful student learning. North Thurston Public Schools Partners in Education (PIE) program helps bring schools and businesses together through volunteering and student support activities. The PIE program is designed as a partnership between a school and business to cultivate positive relationships that benefit education and student learning.
