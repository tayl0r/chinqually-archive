TC United Free Play

Last updated 2019-12-14

TC United’s Friday Free Play and Food Drive

December 13, 2019
6:00pm - 7:30pm
RAC Field 1 in Lacey

Supporting the Thurston County Food Bank

All youth soccer players are invited for a fun night of small sided games!

Players who aren’t currently playing with TC United will need to have their parents fill out a US Club waiver when they arrive at the field.

What to Bring:
Players are encouraged to bring at least 3 food items to donate. Learn more about the items you can bring to donate at https://thurstoncountyfoodbank.org/donating-food/.
