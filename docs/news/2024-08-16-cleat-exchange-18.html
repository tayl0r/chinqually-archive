Cleat exchange!

Last updated 2024-08-16

CLEAT EXCHANGE!

Need a pair of soccer cleats? Have a pair to pass on? Friday, August 16 from 3:00 to 6:00, Chinqually Booters Soccer Club will be hosting a soccer cleat exchange at Wembley Soccer Shop! We will be set up outside in the back of the store. Also, Wembley's is offering a 10% discount on your purchase, including sale and clearance items, if you bring in a pair of gently used soccer cleats between now and Friday, August 16! Please join us and consider bringing soccer cleats or shin guards to pass along.

Wembley Soccer Shop is also having a back-to-school and fall soccer sale - stop in and check it out!
