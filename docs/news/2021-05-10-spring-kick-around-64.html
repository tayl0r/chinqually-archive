Free Clinic

TC United is happy to offer free small-side games for recreational and competitive players seeking to increase game performance and their ball mastery skills in the sport they love. TC United coaches and staff will help develop individual players to develop skills and game understanding while getting to have fun playing in matches. This is a great way to get players excited to play more soccer!

If possible we do ask your child come equipped with a labeled soccer ball, appropriate footwear and cleats.  MASKS WILL ALSO BE REQUIRED FOR ALL PARTICIPANTS AND WE ASK YOU LIMIT ONE ADULT SPECTATOR PER CHILD! We also will be emphasizing fun, so make sure your player brings a smile
